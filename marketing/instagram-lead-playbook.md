# Taprizz — Instagram lead playbook (@taprizz.in)

Goal: turn Jaipur cafe, salon and premium-store owners into WhatsApp leads, then free demos, then plaque sales.
Funnel: Reel/Story → profile → `taprizz.in/ig?src=bio` → WhatsApp / demo / lead form.

## 1. Profile setup (do this first)
- Name: `Taprizz | Google Review Plaques`
- Bio (≤150 chars):
  ```
  More 5★ Google reviews with 1 tap ⚡
  NFC + QR plaque · Gemini AI
  📍 Jaipur · Cafes, salons, stores
  👇 Free live demo
  ```
- Link: `https://taprizz.in/ig?src=bio`
- Switch to a Professional (Business) account, category "Marketing Agency" or "Product/Service", add WhatsApp contact button and city.
- Highlights: Demo · How it works · Pricing · Jaipur clients · FAQ.
- Pin 3 posts: the demo reel, "how it works", a client result (once you have one).

## 2. Content pillars (4 posts/week + daily Stories)
1. **Demo reels** (best for leads): 15–30s showing tap → questions → Gemini draft → posted review. End with "Comment DEMO".
2. **Owner pain**: "Your cafe has 4.1★ and 80 reviews. The one next door has 600." Then the fix.
3. **Education**: how Google reviews affect Maps ranking, what to reply to bad reviews, NFC vs QR.
4. **Social proof**: plaque installed at a Jaipur venue, before/after review counts (only real numbers, with permission).
5. **Behind the scenes**: making the plaques in Jaipur.

## 3. Reel hooks to test
- "Stop begging customers for Google reviews."
- "This 2-inch plaque got a cafe more reviews than ever" (only if true)
- "How Jaipur cafes will win Google Maps in 2026"
- "Tap. Answer. Done. Review posted in 30 seconds."
- "Why your customers love you but never review you"

## 4. Comment-to-DM funnel
CTA on every reel: **"Comment DEMO and I'll send the live demo."**
Use Instagram's keyword auto-reply (or reply manually) with:
```
Hi {name}! Here's the live demo: https://reviews.taprizz.in/s/001
Tap it on your phone, it takes 30 seconds. Which business do you run?
```

## 5. DM scripts
**Warm reply (after DEMO comment)**
```
Thanks for your interest! Did you try the demo? What kind of business do you have and in which area of Jaipur? I'll show how it would look for you.
```
**Cold outreach to a local cafe/salon (personalised, max 15–20 a day, no spam)**
```
Hi! Loved your {specific thing, e.g. cold coffee photos}. I noticed your Google listing has {X} reviews. We built a tap-to-review plaque for Jaipur cafes. Can I send a 30 sec demo?
```
**Follow-up after 2 days**
```
Quick follow-up: I can set up a free sample review page for {business name}. Want me to send it?
```
**Move to WhatsApp**
```
Easier to share details on WhatsApp: +91 96729 99166. Message "Hi" and I'll send pricing and the demo.
```

## 6. Stories (daily, 3–5 frames)
- Poll: "Do you check Google reviews before visiting a place? Yes / Always"
- Quiz: "How many reviews does the top cafe in your area have?"
- Question box: "Ask me anything about Google reviews"
- Link sticker → `taprizz.in/ig?src=story`
- Always add a WhatsApp CTA frame.

## 7. Hashtags (5–8 per post)
`#JaipurCafes #JaipurFoodies #JaipurSalon #JaipurBusiness #GoogleReviews #LocalSEO #SmallBusinessIndia #NFC`

## 8. Weekly routine (about 5 hours)
- Mon: plan content, film 3 reels.
- Tue–Sat: post, reply to every comment/DM within an hour, send 15 personalised outreach DMs.
- Sun: check Insights (reach, profile visits, link clicks, DMs) and the leads in `/admin`. Double down on the top 2 posts.

## 9. Tracking
Leads from `taprizz.in/ig` appear in Admin → Leads with `[source: instagram-bio]`, `instagram-reel`, `instagram-story`, `instagram-dm`.
Use `?src=reel` in reel captions, `?src=story` for link stickers, `?src=dm` in DMs.
Targets for the first 30 days: 12 posts, 100 DMs/conversations, 30 demo views, 10 leads, 2 paid pilots.

## 10. Rules
- Never offer rewards or discounts to customers for positive reviews. It breaks Google's policy.
- Only publish real client results, with the owner's permission.
- Keep outreach personal. Mass copy-paste DMs get accounts restricted.
